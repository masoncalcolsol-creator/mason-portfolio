# UMBRA UAS-001 // Autonomous Emergency Observation

Status: CANONICAL PILOT SPEC v0.1
Dependency: EMS-001 mission model

## Objective

Provide an independent aerial observation layer for an authorized emergency mission so commanded/system-reported state can be compared with observed physical reality.

The UAS is another governed capability under UMBRA, not a privileged controller.

## Initial architecture

AMBULANCE / VEHICLE GATEWAY -> UMBRA MISSION -> UAS ADAPTER -> AUTHORIZED UAS

For prototyping, an Android device may act as the vehicle gateway and publish authenticated GNSS position, heading, velocity, mission state, and health telemetry. The aircraft should follow the authorized mission/envelope rather than blindly following raw phone coordinates.

## Observation envelope

Policy defines a bounded flight/observation volume based on:

- mission identity and authority
- vehicle route and predicted position
- approved operating area
- altitude constraints
- lateral/longitudinal offset
- airspace restrictions
- weather limits
- aircraft health and reserve
- communications health
- crewed-aircraft/conflict handling
- privacy/data-retention rules
- regulator/municipal operating authorization

## Required states

- STANDBY
- ASSIGNED
- PREFLIGHT
- AUTHORIZED
- LAUNCH
- INTERCEPT
- OBSERVE
- REPOSITION
- RETURN
- LAND
- ABORT

## Progressive autonomy ladder

1. Human remote pilot flies; UMBRA records WOULD_FLY guidance.
2. UMBRA generates bounded guidance/waypoints; qualified remote pilot remains responsible and can intervene.
3. Controlled automated launch/follow/recovery in approved environment.
4. Regulator- and municipality-approved operational deployment.
5. Distributed UAS nodes/docks assigned dynamically to missions.

## Independent verification thesis

`COMMAND != REALITY`

For example:

- infrastructure telemetry: corridor segment reports cleared
- UAS observation: residual queue remains in protected envelope
- UMBRA verification: discrepancy event generated
- receipt: commanded state, reported state, observed state, uncertainty, and resolution retained

The UAS therefore functions as an independent truth sensor rather than merely promotional video capture.

## Distributed-node future

A UAS does not need to live on the ambulance. UMBRA may select an eligible aircraft/dock based on mission location, aircraft capability, operating authorization, battery, weather, airspace, and estimated intercept time.

This enables a future municipal robotic infrastructure model in which UAS nodes negotiate bounded capabilities through the same UMBRA architecture used by traffic infrastructure.

## Safety and governance

Actual operations must comply with applicable FAA authorization, remote-pilot requirements, airspace restrictions, municipal policy, emergency-scene restrictions, privacy requirements, and any required coordination with public-safety aviation/UAS operations.

UMBRA software capability does not itself constitute flight authorization.

Loss of mission authority, required telemetry, required communications, geofence compliance, or other policy-defined safety prerequisites must result in the approved degraded/abort behavior.

## Combined EMS-001 demonstration

ONE AMBULANCE / ONE CORRIDOR / ONE DRONE / ONE RECEIPT

The UAS observation timeline should synchronize with vehicle telemetry, traffic-system telemetry, UMBRA events, controller logs, and ground observations to reconstruct the entire experiment.
