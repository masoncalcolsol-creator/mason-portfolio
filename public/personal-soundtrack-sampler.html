PROVENANCE + PROTECTION

A track should have a paper trail.

We can attach cryptographic hashes, timestamps, creator and rights metadata, license records, release identifiers, approved-user scope, and immutable or blockchain-backed provenance records so the origin and authorized use of the asset are easier to prove and audit.

Hashed masterTimestamped provenanceRights recordLicense receiptApproved usersDistribution metadataAudit trail

Provenance technology does not physically prevent copying. It creates evidence: what the original asset was, when it existed, who controlled it, what rights were granted, and whether a later use falls outside that grant. Exclusive rights are defined by the actual agreement and applicable law.
