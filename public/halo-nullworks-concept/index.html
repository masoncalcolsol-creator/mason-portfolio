NULLWORKS treatment

The interface is not the intelligence. It is the seam.

The visual upgrade does not turn Halo into a crowded dashboard. It gives the product a deeper system identity while preserving the clean, wearable-first experience.

01 / PENUMBRA

Halo

The visible layer: camera, display, microphones, bone-conduction audio, and compact prompts placed directly in the wearer’s field of attention.

02 / TRICORDER

Phone

The field computer: connectivity, speech, local processing, sensor fusion, encrypted cache, model routing, and detailed review.

03 / UMBRA

Agent fabric

The invisible work: retrieve the right context, compare evidence, preserve uncertainty, enforce permissions, and generate the next useful action.

04 / HIVE

Durable memory

Original evidence, AI interpretation, human correction, decisions, and final outcomes remain separate, sourced, and receipted.

  confidence: nextAction.confidence,
  source: nextAction.source,
